How the operating model works

When reality changes, the workflow changes with it.

Phox Health supplies the healthcare context and operating authority. Vulpine turns that knowledge into policy, risk, recovery, and proof. Onfleet or another selected provider carries out the delivery plan.

Choose an operational exception.

Exception detected

A routine request becomes critical at 4:45 PM.

Two jobs compete for one driver. One clinic closes at 5:00 PM; the other receiving site operates around the clock.

Operational result

Vulpine protects the irreversible pickup window, recalculates the second job, applies an approved contingency, and records why the decision changed.

  1. 01
    Phox Health

    Context enters

    Request, service expectations, facility hours, receiving point, and known contingency.

  2. 02
    Vulpine

    Consequence evaluated

    Closing time makes the first pickup non-recoverable even though its original label says routine.

  3. 03
    Vulpine

    Network protected

    The assignment is ranked against travel time, coverage, existing work, and the second job’s recoverability.

  4. 04
    Execution

    Plan carried out

    The selected internal driver or Onfleet workflow receives the assignment and updated service instruction.

  5. 05
    Vulpine

    Outcome proved

    Both timelines, the reason for prioritization, the customer update, and any approved exception remain auditable.

The boundary that keeps this defensible

Vulpine does not recreate commodity routing or driver primitives. It preserves a provider-neutral job, policy, evidence, and recovery record so Phox Health can use Onfleet without making Onfleet the operating model.
