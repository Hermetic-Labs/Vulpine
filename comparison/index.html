Routing

Cold chain

Extend
Temperature sensing and temperature-excursion reporting.Planned

Combines sensor trend, ETA, handoff time, and a versioned safety margin into remaining safe-route minutes.

Integration principle

Modernize around the core—not through it.

Vulpine can sit beside existing intake, tracking, and evidence systems. Versioned adapters translate source events into one operational vocabulary while current clients continue working.
